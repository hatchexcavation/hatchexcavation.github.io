Bridgton's Premier Excavation Professionals


     In 1967, John Bosworth and Robert Hatch started a construction business called Better Homes.  They specialized in home building, remodeling and repairs.  Over the years, they plowed snow and the company grew.  In 1996, they incorporated and the business name changed to Bosworth and Hatch, Inc.  The business continued to build homes and expanded to include backhoe work.  In 2007, John retired after forty years of building, Robert continues to work with his son Mark, as a business partner.  After John's retirement, the company changed it's name to accurately account for the work that is being done today...Hatch Excavation Inc.
